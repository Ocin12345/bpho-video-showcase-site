Three distinctions

What the knobs actually change

These are the ideas worth saying aloud in the video.

01

Frequency unlocks emission

Below f0, no individual photon has enough energy to overcome Φ. Increasing brightness cannot repair that deficit.

f0 = Φ / h
02

Intensity changes the count

Once emission is allowed, brighter light sends more photons and raises the current. At fixed wavelength it does not raise Kmax or the stopping voltage.

dim bright
03

Reverse bias reveals energy

A reverse collector voltage turns back even the fastest emitted electrons. When the current reaches zero, eVs equals their maximum kinetic energy.

eVs = Kmax
The final frame

One slope. Different thresholds.
