Task 01

Two-dimensional random walk

Run the walk and compare its final displacement with rRMS = s√N.

Method

θi ∼ U(0, 2π)

Δxi = s cos θi, Δyi = s sin θi

(xi, yi) = (xi−1, yi−1) + (Δxi, Δyi)

⟨r2⟩ = Ns2 ⇒ rRMS = s√N

Each step has fixed length (s), while its direction is sampled uniformly from 0 to 2π.

Large-N Gaussian approximation

Reproducibility controls

Keep the model fixed in two dimensions while changing the animation speed or seed for a repeatable run.
